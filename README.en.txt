Get the most out of your Adlar Castra Aurora heat pump, directly from Homey Pro and over your own network. The app works with the Aurora I/II via Tuya or Modbus and with the Aurora III via Modbus. Follow temperatures, operating state, energy use and efficiency with live monitoring, set setpoints and the heating curve, and automate everything with Homey Flows.

To go further, switch on adaptive control. It learns how your home warms up and cools down, and heats smarter by taking energy prices, the weather and the efficiency of the heat pump into account. Guides for installation, connection and every feature are on the source code page of this app.
